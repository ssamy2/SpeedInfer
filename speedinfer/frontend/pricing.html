FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about billing.

How does SpeedInfer handle credit reservations?

Before dispatching inference to a worker, the gateway reserves a conservative balance based on your requested max_tokens. When the response finishes, SpeedInfer authoritatively settles against the exact tokens reported by the runtime engine and immediately refunds unconsumed credits to your balance.

What payment methods are supported?

Prepaid credit top-ups are processed securely via certified PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways (supporting credit cards, debit cards, and Apple Pay). Enterprise custom invoicing with wire transfers is available for monthly volumes over $2,000.

Are failed or interrupted requests billed?

No. If a model worker experiences an unrecoverable failure or disconnects mid-stream, the reservation is released and your balance is not charged for unserved completions.

Can I host my fine-tuned LoRA checkpoints?

Yes. Custom LoRA adapters fine-tuned on SpeedInfer or uploaded through S3-compatible buckets can be loaded onto Dedicated Endpoints or hot-swapped for low-latency specialized inference.
